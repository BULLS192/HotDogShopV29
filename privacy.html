Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Camarillo Darts (“the Platform”), owned by Omnidite Advisory LLC, respects your privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on usage, the Platform may collect:

  • Player names, nicknames, gender (for tournament use)
  • Tournament results, statistics, and performance history
  • Contact information (email or phone), if provided voluntarily
  • Technical logs for debugging and security

2. How We Use Information

  • To manage tournaments, brackets, payouts, and statistics
  • To generate leaderboards and historical data for players
  • To notify users of tournament updates (future features, e.g. Telegram bot)
  • To improve the Platform’s performance and reliability

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared only with:

  • Tournament directors authorized to manage a specific event
  • Technical service providers (hosting, analytics)
  • Law enforcement if required by law

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards. No method of transmission is 100% secure, and users accept this risk.

5. Data Retention

Players’ stats and history may be retained to support long-term analytics and performance tracking unless a deletion request is made.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Platform is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact privacy@omniditeadvisory.com.
