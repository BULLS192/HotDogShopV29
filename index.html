Step 6 – Payouts

Buy-in, Honey Pot & Mystery Out Pot

Mystery Out is taken out of each player's entry fee. Example: 12 players × $10 entry with $2 Mystery Out per player = $120 player pot and $24 base Mystery Out. If the bar matches the main pot, the total pot becomes $240. If the bar also matches the Mystery Out pot, the total Mystery Out becomes $48 and the remaining tournament payout pool is $240 − $48 = $192.

Percentage Split (of tournament prize pool after Honey Pot and total Mystery Out)

These percentages apply only to the tournament prize pool. Mystery Out is calculated separately from the per-player Mystery Out value above, and both the player-funded and bar-matched Mystery Out amounts are removed from the payout pool.

Optional Fixed Payout Amounts ($)

If you enter a value here, it overrides the percentage-based amount for that position. Use Suggested Even Payouts to round payouts into easy whole numbers that split cleanly between two players.
